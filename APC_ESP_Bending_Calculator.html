2. Calculation Suite Interactive

How to Use This Calculator

This tool implements the 4 industry-standard methods for ESP bending analysis in deviated wells, per Alkhorayef Bulletin TB-GOS-ESP-DEV-001 Rev. 0. Each tab addresses one calculation.

  1. Configure inputs above — casing, tubing, ESP series, material. All downstream calculations use this state.
  2. Max Allowable Length — enter target DLS, get max L from all 4 methods with a chart.
  3. Stress Analysis — enter DLS, get bending stress σ_b with traffic-light banding (Recommended / Caution / HiStress).
  4. DLS Calculator — enter multi-station survey (MD, Inc, Az), get DLS per station via minimum curvature (API RP 78).
  5. Methods & Equations — reference documentation for each formula with derivation and applicable conditions.
Compliance Note
All 4 methods are applied in parallel per Alkhorayef standard practice. When methods diverge, the most conservative (shortest max L / highest σ_b) governs. For any DLS > 6°/100 ft, FEA (ASAP) is mandatory regardless of closed-form results (TB §8.2 Trigger 2).

How to Read This Chart
The gold dashed horizontal line marks your actual ESP length. For any DLS on the x-axis: if your ESP length is below a method's curve → PASSES for that method. If it's above → FAILS (excessive bending). Vertical reference lines mark bulletin-critical DLS thresholds (1°, 3°, 6°/100 ft). Methods can diverge significantly (up to 30-40%); the conservative one governs.

Method 1 — APC L_a (Dr. Lee, January 2023)

Ref: TB-GOS-ESP-DEV-001 §7.3  |  APC Application Engineering internal derivation

Formula La = 0.1667 × √[(ID − OD) × (137,580 / DLS − ID + OD)]     [feet]

Basis: Uses nominal casing ID. Assumes rigid ESP body deflection to contact both ends against nominal ID walls of a curved section. Constant 0.1667 embeds unit conversions (DLS in °/100 ft, dims in inches).

When to use: Primary Alkhorayef method for initial screening; conservative in most cases relative to drift-based methods.

Metric form: La = 0.0508 × √[(ID − OD) × (137,580 / DLS − ID + OD)]    [meters], both ID/OD in inches, DLS in °/100 ft.

Method 2 — Camilleri / SLB (Eqs. 1, 2, 5.2)

Ref: SPE-74560 (Camilleri 2002)  |  SPE-181225 (Camilleri 2016)  |  Baker Hughes ASAP methodology

Eq. 1 — Zero-bend length Lzero = 2 × √[(R + IDeff/2)² − (R − IDeff/2 + OD)²]     [inches → convert to ft]
Eq. 2 — Residual bend transferred to ESP DLSESP = DLScasing − DLSzero
Eq. 5.2 — Bending stress σb = (E × OD × DLSESP) / 137,510     [psi],  E = 30×10⁶ psi steel

Basis: Uses drift × (1 − ovality%) as effective inner clearance. R = 68,754.94 / DLS (radius in inches). Standard SPE ovality assumption: 3%. Accounts for real casing geometry, not idealized nominal ID.

When to use: Rigorous stress calculation. Baker Hughes ASAP FEA uses this framework. Most widely accepted industry method.

Method 3 — PCM Skoczylas

Ref: SPE-179121 (Skoczylas)

Formula (solve for L) L = √[1,500 × (ID − OD) / DLS]     [feet]

Basis: Simplified moment-balance approach. Uses nominal ID. Constant 1,500 embeds unit conversions and material stiffness assumption for ESP-typical geometry.

When to use: Quick sanity check. Generally less conservative than APC or SLB for large ΔD.

Method 4 — LEX (arcsin)

Ref: LEX ESP Cross Training (historical Legacy ESP methodology)

Formula (as function of L) DLS = 2 × arcsin[ 40 × (Drift − OD) / (4 × (Drift − OD)² + L²) ]    [°/100 ft]
Solved for L L = √[ 40 × (Drift − OD) / sin(DLS/2) − 4 × (Drift − OD)² ]    [feet]

Basis: Geometric method using drift diameter. Trigonometric formulation from legacy LEX training material. Similar conservatism to Camilleri/SLB but without ovality treatment.

When to use: Cross-validation against Camilleri/SLB. Common in older engineering practice.

Minimum Curvature (DLS from Survey)

Ref: API RP 78  |  SPE textbook standard

Dogleg angle cos(β) = cos(I₂ − I₁) − sin(I₁) × sin(I₂) × [1 − cos(A₂ − A₁)]
DLS DLS = β × (NormLen / ΔMD) × (180 / π)    where NormLen = 100 ft or 30 m

Note: β must be converted to degrees before scaling. Values I₁, I₂ (inclination) and A₁, A₂ (azimuth) all in the same angle unit (degrees or radians consistently). This calculator internally handles unit conversion.

Bending Stress — Common Framework

Universal bending stress σb = (E × OD × DLSESP) / 137,510    [psi]

Where:
  • E = Elastic modulus (30×10⁶ psi for steel, 29.4×10⁶ for Inconel 718)
  • OD = ESP outer diameter (inches)
  • DLSESP = residual bending on ESP body (°/100 ft) = DLScasing − DLSzero
  • 137,510 = unit conversion constant (embeds π/180 and 100 ft → 1200 in scale)

Standing Principle — Multi-Method Consensus
No single closed-form method captures all real-world effects. Alkhorayef practice applies all 4 in parallel: if 3 agree and 1 diverges by >30%, the divergent result is scrutinized (input error or edge case). The most conservative result governs unless FEA (ASAP) supersedes. For DLS > 6°/100 ft, closed-form is screening only — FEA is mandatory.
